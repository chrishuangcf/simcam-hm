Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This privacy notice for Chris Huang (“we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how SimCam, and this website, handle information. SimCam is a camera for iPhone. It simulates the handling of cameras. The pictures stay with you.

Questions or concerns? Reading this notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. Email photodesignch@gmail.com.

Summary of key points

  • What personal information do we process? You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use SimCam, and the app does not send your photos, film rolls, or camera settings to us.
  • Do we process sensitive personal information? Location can be written into a photo you take, and only if you leave GPS on. Face ID, if you use it, is handled by Apple to unlock the private library. We never receive your location, your biometrics, or the contents of that library.
  • Do we collect information from third parties? No. We do not buy or receive information about you from anyone else.
  • How do we process your information? On the iPhone, the app uses the camera, the microphone while you record video, and, if you allow them, Photos and location, so it can take the picture and keep it where you asked. If you email us, we use that message to reply.
  • How do we keep it safe? The private library and film rolls are encrypted on the device, and can be locked with Face ID. We do not hold a copy. No storage technology can be guaranteed perfect, and deleting the app removes what was only on that phone.
  • What are your rights? Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Because SimCam does not upload your library, those photos are deleted on the device, in Photos, or in Files, by you.

What this notice covers

  • The SimCam app for iPhone.
  • This website, and any page of ours that links to this notice.

What stays on your iPhone

SimCam uses the camera to preview and to take photos and video. Sound is recorded only into videos you choose to record. If you save to Apple Photos, the pictures go into your library, in a SimCam album. If you save to the private library, the photos and finished film rolls stay in an encrypted store on the phone. Settings, including the look, the body, and the rolls you have loaded, stay there too.

GPS is optional. When it is on, the place the photo was taken is written into that photo’s metadata. Turn it off in the camera menu, under GPS. The app does not keep a separate location log and does not send that place to us.

A look you import, and a roll you export, stay in files you control. Sharing or AirDrop is something you start. We do not receive the zip, the contact sheet, or the HTML page.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, advertising identifier, or a profile of how you shoot. SimCam does not show ads, does not include third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. There is no account and nothing to buy inside the app. If you later download SimCam from the App Store, Apple handles that download under its own privacy policy, and we do not see your payment details or Apple ID.

This website

The pages here explain the app. They do not ask you to sign in. If the site is hosted for you to read, the host may keep ordinary server logs, such as an IP address and the page requested. Those logs are the host’s, under its own policy.

Children

SimCam does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no accounts, no ads, no chat or social features, and no third-party tracking. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, email the address below.

Changes to this notice

If SimCam ever changes what it collects, we will update this page and the date above before that change goes live. Continuing to use the app after an update means you have the new notice.
