Support

Need a hand with SimCam? Start with the answers below. If you are still stuck, we are happy to help.

Contact us

Email photodesignch@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot helps too. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

What is SimCam for?

It is a simulation of handling a camera. You work the exposure triangle, focus with the assistants a serious body gives you, build a night exposure, or load a roll of film onto a chosen camera. The photograph is what that handling earns. It stays close to the physical world, and it stays a simulation.

Where do my photos go?

You choose. Save to Apple Photos and they land in your library, in a SimCam album. Save to the private library and they stay inside the app. A finished photo can be HEIF, JPEG, or PNG, and you can keep a RAW or ProRAW file beside it when the phone allows that format.

How does the private library stay private?

Rolls and private shots are encrypted on the iPhone. Face ID can keep that library locked. Nothing in it is uploaded. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if the app is deleted.

Can I leave location out of a photo?

Yes. GPS is optional. When it is on, the place is written into that photo only. Turn it off in the camera menu, under GPS.

How does Night work?

Under one second, Night takes a single photo. From one second, it stacks frames: Average when you want a still picture, Trails when the moving lights should stay. In the hand the shutter runs to 7 seconds. On a tripod, to 30. STD saves a 12 MP HDR HEIF. Hi Res uses the lens’s larger photo where the phone has one.

How do I finish a roll and see it on a computer?

Finish the roll and it stays in the library, marked finished, with a contact sheet. Save Sheet and Share Sheet keep the sheet image. Export Web Page builds a zip of that sheet, the original photos, and an HTML page. Send the zip to a computer, unzip it, and open index.html in a normal browser. Tap a frame to open that photo.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign in to, and nothing to buy inside SimCam. Progress and pictures that live only in the app stay on that iPhone.

I deleted the app and my rolls are gone.

The private library and any roll that was only inside SimCam are removed with the app. We are not able to restore them. Photos you saved to Apple Photos or to Files are still in those places.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone. Phones differ, so a simulated aperture, a night stack, or a lens step follows what that phone can actually capture.

The camera looks wrong, or a shot failed.

Close other camera apps, reopen SimCam, and make sure the phone is on a current iOS. If it continues, email us with the model and what you were shooting: Night, Portrait, video, or a film roll.
